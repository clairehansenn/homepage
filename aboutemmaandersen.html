About Emma Andersen

Emma Andersen is a pop/folk singer-songwriter with about 120,000 monthly listeners on Spotify. She has lived in many different cities, including Bend, Boston, San Diego, and Nashville, where she attended two years of college at Belmont University. Before she finished college, her single "FTSG" gained traction on TikTok with nearly 13,000 videos under the sound. The release of this song in September of 2025 led her to a record deal with RCA Records, which is one of the biggest record labels in the country. With a large record label to support her, she has since been able to produce hundreds of unreleased songs, release an EP, open for two tours, release two music videos, and more. But, RCA is not the only group fueling her success. Her fans are incredibly supportive, and their passion for her music drives a huge part of her music promotion. Her popularity increases everytime a fan posts about her or encourages someone to attend a show. As a fan of Andersen myself, I have actually created a tour headquarters for her, and have since gotten the opportunity to work more directly with her in order to promote her shows and songs. I go more into detail about this under the 'About My Experience' tab.
