How have fan run social media pages contributed to your growth as an artist?

I am honored to have fans that decide to use their free time to support me and my work. I am just so thankful for them and all of their work is so beautiful. I mean, they are always making setlist graphics, posting Tiktoks that make me tear up, and willing to help in any shape or form. Their accounts help create a space for other fans to find updates, and ultimately keep everyone in the loop about what is going on with my career. They also make a lot of trends that tend to gain traction and help my music out a lot. I love them so much and support them all as much as I can.

How do you think social media creates connection between you and your fans?

Social media is one of the most important aspects of my promotion as an artist. The social media community that is present in my fanbase is just such a safe space for all of us to share our thoughts and ideas. One of my favorite aspects of social media is going live on Tiktok. I am able to show fans new music and sit and chat with them in the comments in a low-stress environment. They are so important to me and being able to share spaces with them even while I am not on the road is very special.
